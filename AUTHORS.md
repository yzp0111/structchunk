## Contributors

Thank you to everyone who has contributed to structchunk! This list is
maintained by the project lead; add yourself when making your first pull
request (see below).

## How to Add Yourself

When you open your first pull request, add a bullet to the **Contributors**
section above in alphabetical order. The bullet format is:

    - [your-name](https://github.com/your-handle) — what you contributed — [email](mailto:you@example.com) (optional)

The project lead will merge your PR and the contribution will be reflected
in the next release notes.
